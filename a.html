Discover Our Project ↓

About Our Project

SABIS Recycle is a student-focused recycling website that combines education, games, quizzes, videos, and an AI assistant to help students learn more about responsible recycling.

♻️ What Is SABIS Recycle?

Our goal is to make learning about recycling more interesting than simply reading a list of facts.

The website gives students different ways to learn. They can explore recycling information, watch an educational video, ask the recycling AI questions, and complete a quiz.

The main idea is simple: Learn → Practice → Recycle

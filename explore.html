The 3 Rs ♻️

The easiest way to remember responsible waste management is the 3 Rs: Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle.

📉

Reduce

Use fewer resources and create less waste. Think before buying something you do not really need.

🔄

Reuse

Use items again instead of throwing them away. A reusable bottle is a great example.

♻️

Recycle

Put suitable materials into the correct recycling system so they can potentially become new products.

What Can Be Recycled?

Recycling rules can differ by location, but these materials are commonly recyclable in many systems.

📄

Paper

Clean paper and many paper products can be recyclable.

📦

Cardboard

Clean cardboard boxes are commonly accepted.

🥫

Metal

Aluminum and steel cans are commonly recyclable.

🍾

Glass

Glass bottles and jars can often be recycled where glass collection is available.

Recycling Tips 🌱

Small actions can make a difference. Try these habits at school and at home.

1

Use a reusable water bottle instead of buying disposable bottles.

2

Reuse school supplies whenever they are still useful.

3

Keep recyclable materials clean and dry when required by your local recycling system.

4

Check your local recycling rules if you are unsure about an item.

5

Avoid unnecessary packaging when you have a choice.

6

Remember: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle!
